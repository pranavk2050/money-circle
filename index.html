The Circulatory System of the Rupee

How money moves through the Indian economy — a living map of annual flows

Figures are an illustrative, order-of-magnitude model (₹ lakh crore per year), grounded in approximate FY2024–25 public data — RBI Annual Report & Monetary Policy Report, Union Budget documents, CSO National Accounts, Ministry of Commerce trade statistics and World Bank remittance estimates — recalled from training data rather than freshly looked up. Treat this as a teaching visualization of scale and direction, not an audited national-accounts reconciliation.
